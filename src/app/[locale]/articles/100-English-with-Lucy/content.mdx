## Greetings and conversation starters

1. **Hey, how are you?**
2. **How's everything going?**
3. **It's a pleasure to meet you.**
4. **Long time no see.**
5. **How have you been?**
6. **What are you up to these days?**

## Replying to greetings

7. **I'm really good, thank you.**
8. **I'm doing well, thanks.**
9. **Not too bad.**
10. **Lovely to meet you too.**
11. **How about you?**
12. **Not much, the usual really.**

## Saying goodbye

13. **I must be making a move.**
14. **I need to get going.**
15. **I'm going to have to make an exit.**
16. **Speak soon.**
17. **See you later.**
18. **It was so nice to meet you.**
19. **Hope to hear from you soon.**

## Buying time to think

20. **I'm going to have to have a think about that.**
21. **Let me have a think about that.**
22. **Give me a moment.**
23. **Now that's a good question.**

## Asking someone to repeat themselves

24. **Sorry.**
25. **Pardon.**
26. **Excuse me.**
27. **Could you repeat that for me?**
28. **Would you mind saying that again?**
29. **Oh, I didn't quite catch that.**

## When you do not understand

30. **Oh, I'm not really sure.**
31. **I don't have a clue.**
32. **I have no idea, sorry.**

## Asking for an opinion

33. **What's your take on this?**
34. **What do you think?**
35. **What are your thoughts on this?**

## Giving an opinion

36. **In all honesty, I don't think...**
37. **If you ask me...**
38. **The way I see it...**
39. **From my point of view...**

## Agreeing

40. **You are totally right.**
41. **I completely agree with you.**
42. **I feel exactly the same way.**

## Disagreeing

43. **I don't quite agree with you on this one.**
44. **Actually, I don't really agree.**
45. **I beg to differ.**
46. **That's not always the case.**

## Making suggestions

47. **How about we go out tonight?**
48. **What do you think about staying in?**
49. **That sounds like a plan.**
50. **Seems good to me.**

## Saying no

51. **I don't think I fancy that.**
52. **I don't really feel like it.**
53. **I'm not sure about that.**

## Asking for permission

54. **Do you mind if we leave?**
55. **Would it be okay if we stayed?**
56. **Can I possibly go?**
57. **Could I possibly go?**

## Giving or refusing permission

58. **Of course, I don't mind.**
59. **Of course you can.**
60. **Please, go ahead.**
61. **I'm going to have to say no.**
62. **Unfortunately, I can't let you do that.**
63. **I'm sorry, but I do mind.**

## Saying thank you

64. **Thank you so much.**
65. **I really appreciate that.**
66. **I'd really like to thank you.**
67. **I can't thank you enough.**

## Responding to thanks

68. **You're welcome.**
69. **My pleasure.**
70. **Oh, anytime.**
71. **Don't mention it.**

## Apologizing

72. **I'm so sorry.**
73. **I'd like to apologize.**
74. **Please accept my sincerest apologies.**

## Responding to an apology

75. **You're forgiven.**
76. **That's okay.**
77. **Oh, no worries.**

## Making requests

78. **Would you mind slowing down?**
79. **Would you be able to slow down?**
80. **Do you think you could slow down?**
81. **Could you slow down?**
82. **Can you slow down?**
83. **I was wondering if you could slow down.**

## Making an offer

84. **Can I help you?**
85. **How about a drink?**
86. **Would you like me to go?**

## Responding to an offer

87. **Oh yes, please.**
88. **That would be wonderful, thank you.**
89. **Oh, it's okay, but thank you.**
90. **No, thank you.**

## Asking for contact details

91. **Please could I have your contact details?**
92. **Can I take your card?**
93. **Is there any way I can contact you?**
94. **How can I get in touch with you?**

## Expressing preferences

95. **I'm a real fan of chocolate.**
96. **I'm very keen on music.**
97. **I'm really fond of him.**
98. **I absolutely love it.**
99. **I'm really not a fan of chocolate.**
100.  **I'm not very keen on music.**
